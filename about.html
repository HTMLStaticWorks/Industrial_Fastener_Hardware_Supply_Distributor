Heritage & Precision Since 1988

Building Trust One Bolt At A Time

Founded in the heart of industrial Cleveland, IndustrialPro Fasteners has grown from a regional specialty bolt warehouse into one of the nation's premier master distributors of heavy structural and engineered fastening systems.

Our Mission

Zero Failure In Every Fastener

When a bridge spans a river, an oil rig withstands hurricane seas, or a high-rise ascends into the skyline, safety rests on the integrity of structural bolts. We operate with zero tolerance for sub-standard fasteners. Every heat lot we distribute is accompanied by verifiable mill certifications, tested tensile loads, and full traceability.

We treat our commercial partners not as invoice numbers, but as vital collaborators in building and manufacturing critical infrastructure.
