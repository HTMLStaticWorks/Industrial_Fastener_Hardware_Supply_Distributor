The FastenCore Heritage

From a Midwest Bolt House to North America's Automation Leader

Founded in 1988, FastenCore grew from a 15,000 sq ft Cleveland warehouse into a 500,000 sq ft automated mega-hub — built on a single uncompromising principle: every fastener must carry certified metallurgical traceability.

In 1988, founder Arthur Sterling Sr. launched FastenCore in Cleveland, Ohio with a modest 15,000 sq ft warehouse and one flatbed truck, driven by an uncompromising standard: every structural fastener must carry certified Mill Test Reports (MTR) and guaranteed metallurgical traceability.

Today, FastenCore operates a flagship 500,000-square-foot automated mega-hub with autonomous ASRS robotics, optical laser AI sorting maintaining an industry-leading < 50 PPM defect threshold, and SmartBin IoT scale replenishment for 250+ manufacturing plants.

"A 50-cent bolt holds together a fifty-million-dollar bridge. Never compromise the bolt, or you endanger the bridge."
