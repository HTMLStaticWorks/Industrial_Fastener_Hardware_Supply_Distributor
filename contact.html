Frequently Asked Questions

Fastener Dispatch & Commercial RFQ Guide

Everything you need to know about bulk pricing, MTR test reports, Net terms, expedited shipments, and custom fabrication.

What is the typical turnaround time for bulk RFQs?

In-stock catalog fastener items (such as ASTM A325 heavy hex bolts, Grade 8 cap screws, and standard 316 stainless hardware) are quoted within 60 minutes during standard commercial hours (6 AM – 7 PM EST). Custom ASTM alloy fabrication, mill-run batches, or specialized Teflon/cadmium coatings are returned within 4 to 24 hours with complete metallurgical specifications.

Are Certified Material Test Reports (MTRs) provided with orders?

Yes. 100% of our structural, petrochemical, aerospace, and high-tensile hardware lots include complete EN 10204 3.1 Certified Material Test Reports (MTRs), chemical heat analysis, proof load verification, and hardness testing data. All documents are permanently archived and accessible 24/7 via the client portal.

What are your delivery transit times and emergency options?

From our regional hubs in Cleveland OH, Houston TX, Atlanta GA, and Reno NV, we provide 24- to 48-hour ground transit across 90% of US job sites. Orders placed before 4:00 PM EST stage for same-day departure. For critical plant shutdowns or job-site delays, we operate a 24/7 dedicated Hot-Shot courier service and chartered air freight options.

How do contractors and plants establish Net 30 or Net 60 commercial credit?

Qualified subcontractors, general contractors, and manufacturing plants can establish Net 30 or Net 60 commercial terms within 24 to 48 hours. Credit lines range from $15,000 to $250,000+ based on trade history. To expedite your application, email your credit sheet and bank references to credit@fastencore.com.

Do you support custom fastener fabrication, threading, and coatings?

Yes. Our in-house CNC lathe and cold-heading cells produce custom head geometries, left-hand threads, non-standard diameters, and exotic superalloys (Inconel 718, Monel 400, Hastelloy, Titanium, and 316L). Protective finishes include Hot-Dip Galvanizing (ASTM A153), Zinc-Nickel, Fluoropolymer/Xylan 1424, and Cadmium plating with comprehensive salt-spray certifications.

Are your hardware products DFARS and Buy America (BAA) compliant?

Yes. 100% domestic melt-and-manufacture fastener stock, DFARS 252.225-7009 qualifying country material, and AIS (American Iron and Steel) compliant certifications are available and tracked at the SKU line level for federal infrastructure, military, and DOT highway contracts.

Have a complex specification or custom drawing? Our metallurgists and fastening application specialists are ready to help.
