Frequently Asked Questions

Instant Dispatch & RFQ Guide
What is the typical turnaround time for bulk RFQs?

In-stock catalog items are quoted within 60 minutes during commercial hours (6 AM – 7 PM EST). Custom ASTM alloy fabrication or mill-run RFQs are returned within 4 to 24 hours with complete metallurgical specs.

Are Certified Material Test Reports (MTRs) provided?

Yes. 100% of our structural, petrochemical, and high-tensile hardware lots include complete EN 10204 3.1 Certified Material Test Reports, heat numbers, and proof-load data downloadable through our portal 24/7.

What are your delivery timeframes and emergency options?

We provide 24- to 48-hour ground transit across 90% of US job sites from OH, TX, GA, and NV. Orders before 4 PM stage same-day. For emergency shutdown situations, 24/7 Hot-Shot dedicated couriers and air freight are available.

How do contractors establish Net 30 or Net 60 credit?

Commercial credit applications are approved within 24–48 hours for qualified subcontractors and EPCs, with revolving credit lines from $15,000 to $250,000+. Email trade references to credit@industrialprofasteners.com.

Do you support custom fastener fabrication & coatings?

Yes. Our CNC and cold-heading facilities produce custom shanks, left-hand threads, and exotic alloys (Inconel, Monel, 316SS). Finishes include Hot-Dip Galvanized (A153), Zinc-Nickel, Fluoropolymer/Xylan, and Cadmium.

Are your products DFARS and Buy America (BAA) compliant?

Yes. 100% USA Melt and Manufacture documentation, DFARS qualifying country compliance, and AIS certifications can be specified at the line-item level for federal highway, infrastructure, and defense jobs.
